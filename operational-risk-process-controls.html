Division 03

Stronger operations begin with understanding how work gets done.

A business can experience operational problems when processes are unclear, responsibilities are poorly defined, records are incomplete or important controls are not consistently applied. Our Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory division helps businesses examine selected operational activities, identify weaknesses and document practical improvements.

Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory

What we help businesses examine

  • Operational risks.
  • Process steps and responsibilities.
  • Documentation and recordkeeping.
  • Internal control activities.
  • Process inconsistencies.
  • Evidence gaps.
  • Corrective action requirements.

Our approach

Assessment based on evidence made available

We assess the agreed process or operational area using information made available by the client, discussions with relevant personnel and other appropriate evidence.

We document observations and distinguish identified weaknesses from matters requiring further verification. Our recommendations are intended to help businesses improve their internal organisation and control practices.

Professional boundary

What these services are not

These services do not constitute legal advice, statutory compliance certification, a statutory audit or an assurance opinion.

Where a matter requires a regulated professional or specialist legal opinion, we may recommend that the client obtain appropriate external assistance.

Important scope note

The register and diagnostic outputs are advisory working documents, not guarantees that every risk has been identified or eliminated. Assessments reflect the information made available during the engagement and matters requiring further verification are clearly distinguished from established findings.
