Business Intelligence & Performance Analytics

Before you analyse the numbers, understand the data.

Business analysis is only as reliable as the information supporting it. A Business Data Diagnostic examines selected business records or datasets to identify problems affecting their organisation, consistency, completeness and suitability for analysis.

What this service addresses

You may have business information but still experience difficulties such as:

  • Records stored across multiple spreadsheets.
  • Inconsistent naming or data formats.
  • Missing values or incomplete entries.
  • Duplicate records.
  • Unclear categories.
  • Reporting figures that do not reconcile.
  • Difficulty preparing information for dashboards or analysis.
What we do

Depending on the agreed scope, we:

  1. Review the supplied datasets and their structure.
  2. Examine completeness and consistency.
  3. Identify duplicates, missing information and apparent anomalies.
  4. Assess whether the data is suitable for the intended analytical purpose.
  5. Organise or clean a sample or agreed dataset where included in scope.
  6. Document findings and recommended improvements.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • Data diagnostic report.
  • Data-quality findings.
  • Summary of identified issues.
  • Agreed cleaned or organised dataset, if included.
  • Recommendations for improving data management.
  • Suggested next steps for reporting or analysis.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • The relevant business datasets or exports.
  • An explanation of how the information is currently used.
  • The business question or reporting objective.
  • Relevant definitions or explanations of the data fields.

Scope considerations

The quotation will identify the datasets, reporting periods, volume and level of data cleaning included. Extensive reconstruction, system integration or ongoing data management is not automatically included.
