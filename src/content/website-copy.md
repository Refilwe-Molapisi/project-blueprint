# Making business information more useful.

Businesses generate and encounter significant amounts of information. However, having information does not automatically mean that a business understands its performance, its market position or the risks affecting its operations.

Our purpose is to help bridge that gap.

**Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory**

We review business processes, operational risks, documentation and controls to identify weaknesses and recommend improvements.

**Research Publications & Intelligence Products**

## Our approach to advisory work

We believe that sound business advice begins with a properly understood question.

Before undertaking an assignment, we establish what the client needs to understand, what information is available, what work is appropriate and what the final output should achieve.

Our work is guided by five principles:

**Evidence before assumption**

We distinguish between verified information, interpretation, estimates and matters that cannot be established from the available evidence.

**Clarity over complexity**

We aim to make findings understandable and relevant to the people who need to use them.

**Defined professional boundaries**

We communicate the scope of our work and do not present advisory outputs as legal opinions, statutory audits, accounting assurance or regulated professional services where these are not within our authorised scope.

**Documented work**

Our engagements are supported by structured working methods and written deliverables.

**Responsible use of information**

We take confidentiality, data handling, source attribution and appropriate access to client information seriously.

## How we deliver our work

We serve clients across South Africa through:

- Remote engagements.
- Hybrid engagements.
- Agreed visits to client premises.
- Appropriate field-based assignments.

The delivery arrangement is determined by the nature of the work, the client's requirements and the information or access needed.

On-site and field-based work is agreed and priced separately where applicable.

## Our commitment

We aim to build a firm recognised for disciplined analysis, professional communication, reliable documentation and useful business insight.

We do not believe every business problem requires a large consulting engagement. We believe the right work should be defined around the actual question, the available evidence and the value the assignment is expected to provide.

# 13. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

## About the firm

**What does [Company Name] do?**

We provide business intelligence, economic and commercial research, performance analysis and operational risk advisory services. We also develop and sell independent research publications.

**Where do you operate?**

We serve clients throughout South Africa.

**Do you only work with large companies?**

No. Our services are designed to support established and growing businesses, including micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.

**Do you have a physical office?**

Our initial operating model is remote-first, with agreed hybrid and on-site engagements at client premises where required. Any formal office details will be published if and when applicable.

## Services

**How do I know which service I need?**

Start by identifying the question you need answered. Internal performance questions generally fall under Business Intelligence & Performance Analytics. External market and commercial questions fall under Economic & Commercial Intelligence. Process, risk and control questions fall under Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory.

If you are uncertain, submit an enquiry.

**Can I request a customised assignment?**

Yes. Requirements that fall outside standard service packages may be assessed and separately scoped.

**Do you work remotely?**

Yes. Remote delivery is available where the nature of the work permits it.

**Can you work at our premises?**

Yes. On-site arrangements may be agreed where required. Travel, accommodation and other applicable fieldwork costs will be disclosed separately.

**Will I receive a written report?**

Written documentation is a standard part of our service approach. The specific format and level of detail depend on the engagement.

**Will you need access to our accounting or business systems?**

Not necessarily. We prefer relevant data exports or authorised read-only access where these are sufficient. Any system access must be agreed, appropriately authorised and limited to the requirements of the engagement.

## Quotations and payments

**How do I obtain a quotation?**

Submit an enquiry describing your requirement. We may arrange an initial discussion before preparing a quotation.

**What is your payment policy?**

Engagements below R2,000 are payable in full upfront. Engagements of R2,000 or more require a 50% deposit and a 50% final payment.

**When does work begin?**

Work begins after the quotation and engagement terms have been accepted, required onboarding information has been received and the applicable initial payment has been confirmed.

**Are travel costs included?**

Not automatically. Any applicable travel, accommodation, fieldwork or external specialist costs will be identified in the quotation.

## Research publications

**What is the difference between a research publication and commissioned research?**

A research publication is developed independently and sold as a standard product to multiple customers. Commissioned research is undertaken for a specific client according to an agreed scope and requirements.

**How do I purchase a publication?**

Select the publication, review its description and licence, and complete the online checkout process.

**When will I receive my publication?**

Secure access is provided after payment has been confirmed and the purchase entitlement has been created.

**Can I share a purchased publication?**

The standard purchase licence does not permit unauthorised redistribution, resale or republication. Refer to the licence displayed for the specific product.

**Can I request research on a specific topic?**

Yes. Submit a commissioned research enquiry through the contact page.

## Confidentiality and information

**How is client information handled?**

Client information is handled according to the applicable engagement terms and the firm's privacy and information-handling procedures.

**Should I send sensitive documents through the contact form?**

No. The general enquiry form should not be used for confidential financial records, passwords or sensitive personal information. Secure document submission arrangements can be established during onboarding.

# 19. FINAL CONTENT AND IMPLEMENTATION RULES

1. Use the approved corporate colours: #136CFC, #152641, #C3F84A and #E8F4FC.
2. Use Libre Baskerville and Montserrat according to the approved typography system.
3. Maintain the editorial, structured and professional visual direction.
4. Do not invent the company name, registration details, address or contact information.
5. Do not invent clients, testimonials, case studies, awards or professional credentials.
6. Do not publish provisional service prices until approved.
7. Do not publish research products that have not been completed and approved.
8. Do not display fake dashboards, sales figures or performance statistics.
9. Do not make legal, tax, audit or regulatory claims outside the firm's actual authority.
10. Do not use generic filler copy to make pages appear longer.
11. Do not make every page look identical. Maintain a consistent design system while giving each page a distinct editorial layout.
12. Every CTA must lead to a real, functioning destination.
13. All forms must validate inputs and provide clear confirmation or error messages.
14. All public-facing content must be editable through the website's content management system where practical.
15. All client and administrative areas must be protected by appropriate authentication and access controls.
16. Every page must be responsive and accessible.
17. Use South African English conventions and ZAR currency formatting.
18. Do not publish draft legal documents as final policies.

**The desired result:** a complete, credible and commercially functional website that explains what the firm does, demonstrates its specialist capabilities, makes it easy to enquire or purchase research products, and provides a foundation for the firm's future digital operations.
