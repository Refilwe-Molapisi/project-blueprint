Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory

Understand the process before trying to improve it.

A business process may appear straightforward but still contain unnecessary steps, unclear responsibilities, inconsistent practices or weak controls. A Process & Controls Diagnostic examines an agreed process to identify how work is performed and where improvements may be appropriate.

What this service addresses

This service is useful when a business has:

  • Processes that are not properly documented.
  • Unclear responsibilities.
  • Repeated administrative work.
  • Inconsistent approval practices.
  • Missing supporting records.
  • Weak or poorly defined control activities.
What we do

Our working process:

  1. Agree the process to be examined.
  2. Review relevant documentation.
  3. Discuss the process with appropriate personnel.
  4. Map the existing process where included.
  5. Identify potential weaknesses and control gaps.
  6. Develop practical improvement recommendations.
  7. Prepare an implementation checklist.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • Process overview or process map.
  • Diagnostic findings.
  • Identified process weaknesses.
  • Control observations.
  • Recommendations.
  • Suggested implementation checklist.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • The process to be examined and its boundaries.
  • Relevant process documents, templates and records.
  • Access to the personnel who perform the process, where agreed.

Scope considerations

The engagement will specify the process, locations, personnel and level of documentation included. Implementing recommended changes, configuring business software or redesigning multiple processes requires separate agreement unless included in the quotation.
