Frequently asked questions

Questions about how we work.

Practical answers about our services, quotations, payments, publications and the handling of client information.

About the firm

What does [Company Name] do?
We provide business intelligence, economic and commercial research, performance analysis and operational risk advisory services. We also develop and sell independent research publications.
Where do you operate?
We serve clients throughout South Africa.
Do you only work with large companies?
No. Our services are designed to support established and growing businesses, including micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.
Do you have a physical office?
Our initial operating model is remote-first, with agreed hybrid and on-site engagements at client premises where required. Any formal office details will be published if and when applicable.

Services

How do I know which service I need?
Start by identifying the question you need answered. Internal performance questions generally fall under Business Intelligence & Performance Analytics. External market and commercial questions fall under Economic & Commercial Intelligence. Process, risk and control questions fall under Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory. If you are uncertain, submit an enquiry.
Can I request a customised assignment?
Yes. Requirements that fall outside standard service packages may be assessed and separately scoped.
Do you work remotely?
Yes. Remote delivery is available where the nature of the work permits it.
Can you work at our premises?
Yes. On-site arrangements may be agreed where required. Travel, accommodation and other applicable fieldwork costs will be disclosed separately.
Will I receive a written report?
Written documentation is a standard part of our service approach. The specific format and level of detail depend on the engagement.
Will you need access to our accounting or business systems?
Not necessarily. We prefer relevant data exports or authorised read-only access where these are sufficient. Any system access must be agreed, appropriately authorised and limited to the requirements of the engagement.

Quotations and payments

How do I obtain a quotation?
Submit an enquiry describing your requirement. We may arrange an initial discussion before preparing a quotation.
What is your payment policy?
Engagements below R2,000 are payable in full upfront. Engagements of R2,000 or more require a 50% deposit and a 50% final payment.
When does work begin?
Work begins after the quotation and engagement terms have been accepted, required onboarding information has been received and the applicable initial payment has been confirmed.
Are travel costs included?
Not automatically. Any applicable travel, accommodation, fieldwork or external specialist costs will be identified in the quotation.

Research publications

What is the difference between a research publication and commissioned research?
A research publication is developed independently and sold as a standard product to multiple customers. Commissioned research is undertaken for a specific client according to an agreed scope and requirements.
How do I purchase a publication?
Select the publication, review its description and licence, and complete the online checkout process.
When will I receive my publication?
Secure access is provided after payment has been confirmed and the purchase entitlement has been created.
Can I share a purchased publication?
The standard purchase licence does not permit unauthorised redistribution, resale or republication. Refer to the licence displayed for the specific product.
Can I request research on a specific topic?
Yes. Submit a commissioned research enquiry through the contact page.

Confidentiality and information

How is client information handled?
Client information is handled according to the applicable engagement terms and the firm's privacy and information-handling procedures.
Should I send sensitive documents through the contact form?
No. The general enquiry form should not be used for confidential financial records, passwords or sensitive personal information. Secure document submission arrangements can be established during onboarding.

Still have a question?

Submit an enquiry describing what you are trying to understand, and we will point you to the right service or publication.
