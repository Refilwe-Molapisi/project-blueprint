Economic & Commercial Intelligence

Understand the relationship between your prices and costs.

Pricing decisions affect revenue, margins and commercial sustainability. A Pricing & Profitability Review examines selected pricing and cost information to help a business understand its current pricing structure and the gross margin implications of alternative scenarios.

What this service addresses

This service is useful when a business has:

  • Uncertainty about gross margins.
  • Difficulty comparing product or service prices.
  • Changes in input costs.
  • Questions about the effect of price adjustments.
  • Limited visibility of product-level or service-level profitability.
What we do

Our working process:

  1. Review supplied pricing and cost information.
  2. Identify relevant cost and revenue components.
  3. Calculate gross margins where the information supports this.
  4. Compare selected products, services or pricing arrangements.
  5. Examine agreed pricing scenarios.
  6. Present findings and commercial considerations.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • Pricing and cost summary.
  • Relevant gross margin calculations.
  • Comparative schedules.
  • Agreed pricing scenarios.
  • Written findings.
  • Practical considerations for management.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • Current selling prices.
  • Relevant direct cost information.
  • Product or service descriptions.
  • Discounts or pricing rules relevant to the analysis.
  • Any specific pricing questions.

Scope considerations

Results depend on the accuracy and completeness of supplied cost information.

Important professional boundary

The review is not a statutory audit, independent valuation, tax opinion or formal financial assurance engagement.
