Economic & Commercial Intelligence

Understand the businesses competing for the same customers.

A Competitor Intelligence Snapshot provides a structured review of selected competitors and relevant publicly available commercial information. It helps a business understand how selected competitors present their products or services, what they offer and how their visible commercial positioning compares.

What this service addresses

This service is useful when a business has:

  • Limited understanding of competitor offerings.
  • Uncertainty about publicly advertised prices.
  • Difficulty comparing products or services.
  • Questions about competitor positioning.
  • A need for a structured competitor overview.
What we do

Our working process:

  1. Define the relevant market or competitor group.
  2. Agree the competitors to be examined.
  3. Identify appropriate information sources.
  4. Review relevant products, services and publicly available prices.
  5. Compare selected features and positioning.
  6. Document findings and commercial observations.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • Competitor overview.
  • Comparison tables.
  • Selected product or service comparisons.
  • Public pricing observations where available.
  • Source references.
  • Key findings and commercial considerations.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • The competitors or market you want examined.
  • Any commercial questions you want answered.
  • Relevant internal observations you want compared against public information.

Scope considerations

The engagement will define the competitors, geographic coverage, information categories and research period. We do not represent publicly available information as a complete view of a competitor's internal financial position, strategy or confidential operations.
