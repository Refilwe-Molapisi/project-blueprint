Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory

Make operational risks visible and easier to manage.

Operational risks can affect service delivery, financial performance, business continuity and the reliability of everyday activities. An Operational Risk Register helps a business document selected risks, assess their significance and identify appropriate control considerations.

What this service addresses

This service is useful when a business has:

  • Risks that have not been formally documented.
  • Unclear responsibility for risk management.
  • Inconsistent operational practices.
  • Limited visibility of control weaknesses.
  • Difficulty tracking corrective actions.
What we do

Our working process:

  1. Agree the operational areas to be considered.
  2. Gather relevant information through documents and discussions.
  3. Identify selected operational risks.
  4. Assess risks using an agreed rating approach.
  5. Document existing controls where information is available.
  6. Identify possible control improvements.
  7. Prepare an action tracker.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • Operational Risk Register.
  • Risk descriptions and categories.
  • Risk assessment ratings.
  • Existing control observations.
  • Proposed control measures.
  • Suggested risk owners.
  • Corrective action tracker.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • The operational areas to be considered.
  • Relevant internal documents and records.
  • Access to relevant personnel for discussions, where agreed.

Scope considerations

The engagement will define the number of risks, business areas and level of assessment. The register is an advisory working document, not a guarantee that every risk has been identified or eliminated.
