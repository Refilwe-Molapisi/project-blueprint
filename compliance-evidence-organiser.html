Operational Risk, Process & Controls Advisory

Bring structure to your business documentation.

Businesses often need to maintain records and supporting documents for internal administration, external requirements or professional review. The Compliance Evidence Organiser helps organise selected documents and identify gaps in the available evidence.

What this service addresses

This service is useful when a business has:

  • Documents stored across different locations.
  • Unclear document ownership.
  • Missing or expired records.
  • Difficulty locating supporting evidence.
  • Inconsistent document organisation.
  • No central document checklist.
What we do

Our working process:

  1. Agree the document categories to be reviewed.
  2. Review the records made available.
  3. Develop an agreed document inventory.
  4. Identify missing or incomplete items.
  5. Organise a document and evidence tracker.
  6. Recommend a practical structure for maintaining records.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • Document inventory.
  • Evidence checklist.
  • Missing-document tracker.
  • Document organisation recommendations.
  • Suggested recordkeeping structure.
  • Referral recommendations where specialist advice is needed.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • The document categories to be reviewed.
  • Access to the records made available for review.
  • Any internal or external requirements the documents must support.

Scope considerations

The engagement will specify the document categories, locations and level of organisation included. This service helps organise documentation and identify apparent evidence gaps.

Important professional boundary

It does not certify legal compliance, determine legal obligations or replace advice from an appropriately qualified legal, tax or regulatory professional.
