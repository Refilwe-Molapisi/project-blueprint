Business Intelligence & Performance Analytics

See your business performance more clearly.

A Business Intelligence Dashboard brings selected business indicators together in a visual reporting environment. Instead of working through multiple records to understand performance, management can review agreed indicators in one structured dashboard.

What this service addresses

This service is useful when a business experiences:

  • Business information spread across multiple files.
  • Difficulty tracking performance indicators.
  • Time-consuming manual reporting.
  • Limited visibility of sales or expense trends.
  • Difficulty comparing reporting periods.
  • Inconsistent management reports.
What we do

Our working process:

  1. Establish the dashboard's purpose and intended users.
  2. Identify the relevant performance indicators.
  3. Review the available data sources.
  4. Agree the dashboard structure and reporting requirements.
  5. Prepare and organise the relevant data.
  6. Develop the dashboard.
  7. Test calculations and visualisations.
  8. Provide handover guidance.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • A dashboard built around the agreed requirements.
  • Agreed KPI definitions.
  • Selected charts, tables and performance indicators.
  • Filters or reporting-period selections where included.
  • Data-source documentation.
  • User guidance or handover notes.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • Relevant business data or system exports.
  • Reporting objectives.
  • Desired performance indicators.
  • Relevant definitions and business rules.
  • Intended dashboard users.

Scope considerations

The quotation will define the data sources, number of indicators, dashboard complexity, update arrangements and software requirements. Ongoing automated connections, system integrations, software licences and continuing maintenance are separate unless expressly included.
