Keep a clearer view of supplier costs.

Supplier prices can change over time, affecting purchasing decisions and business costs. Supplier Cost Watch provides a structured comparison or recurring review of selected supplier prices and cost categories.

What this service addresses

This service is useful when a business has:

  • Limited visibility of supplier price movements.
  • Difficulty comparing supplier quotations.
  • Uncertainty about changes in purchasing costs.
  • Inconsistent supplier comparison records.
  • A need for recurring cost monitoring.
What we do

Depending on the agreed engagement, we:

  1. Identify the suppliers and cost categories to monitor.
  2. Review supplied quotations, price lists or other appropriate information.
  3. Compare selected prices and terms.
  4. Record observed price changes.
  5. Identify notable cost movements.
  6. Present a structured comparison and observations.
What you receive

Deliverables for this service

  • Supplier comparison schedule.
  • Price baseline.
  • Cost movement summary.
  • Relevant supporting source information.
  • Periodic monitoring report where agreed.
  • Management observations.
What we need from you

Inputs that make the work possible

  • The suppliers, products or cost categories you want monitored.
  • Supplied quotations, price lists or purchase records.
  • Any purchasing rules or thresholds relevant to the comparison.

Scope considerations

The quotation will specify the suppliers, items, reporting frequency and sources of information. We do not guarantee supplier prices, product availability or future price movements.
