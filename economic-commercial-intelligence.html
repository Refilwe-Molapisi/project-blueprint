Division 02

Understand the market beyond your business.

Businesses operate within changing commercial environments. Competitor activity, supplier costs, pricing decisions and wider economic conditions can all affect business performance. Our Economic & Commercial Intelligence division investigates relevant external information to help businesses assess their commercial position and make more informed decisions.

Economic & Commercial Intelligence

What we help businesses examine

  • Competitor products and services.
  • Publicly available competitor pricing.
  • Market and industry developments.
  • Supplier prices and cost changes.
  • Pricing structures and gross margin information.
  • Commercial opportunities and constraints.
  • Relevant economic indicators.

Our approach

Relevant, identifiable information — with stated limitations

We use relevant, identifiable information and explain the coverage and limitations of the analysis.

Where information is publicly available, we distinguish observed facts from interpretation. Where information is supplied by the client, we identify the data used and any limitations affecting the findings.

Typical outputs

What clients may receive

  • Competitor comparison tables.
  • Pricing comparison schedules.
  • Supplier comparison reports.
  • Cost movement summaries.
  • Gross margin calculations where supported by supplied information.
  • Commercial observations.
  • Written recommendations and decision considerations.

Important scope note

Where information is publicly available, we do not represent it as a complete view of a competitor's internal financial position, strategy or confidential operations. Where cost analysis depends on supplied information, results depend on the accuracy and completeness of that information.
